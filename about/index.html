About Stride

Local service. Personal accountability.

Stride Exterior Cleaning is a local business working out of Redland Bay, servicing the Redlands and selected parts of Greater Brisbane.

Get a free quote Request a booking

It isn’t a franchise, and it isn’t a call centre. The person who quotes your job is the person who turns up and does it, which means there’s nobody to pass the blame to if it isn’t right.

Stride was started to do exterior cleaning the way it should be done: assess the surface first, choose a method that suits it, communicate clearly, and document the result. That sounds obvious. It’s also noticeably not the industry standard.

There are no review counts or job numbers on this site because they would not yet mean anything. What stands behind the work is $10 million public liability cover, commercial equipment, and a written scope on every job.
