    1. More than dirt

      That green film on the path, and those dark marks on the roof, are more than dirt. They are alive.

    2. What it is

      Algae, mould, lichen and moss are living organisms. They settle wherever a surface stays damp, usually on the shaded side of the house. In a warm, humid climate like South East Queensland, conditions suit them for most of the year.

    3. On the roof

      Lichen and moss anchor into the tile surface, and hold water against it like a sponge. Lichen also releases weak acids that slowly wear the surface. Loose growth then washes down, and can block the gutters.

    4. On walls and paint

      Mould and algae feed on the dirt and organic matter sitting on paint and render. They keep the surface damp for longer, and that moisture can shorten the life of the paint.

    5. Underfoot

      The risk is more immediate. Wet algae forms a slippery film on driveways, paths and decks. And timber that stays damp is timber that can start to rot.

    6. It keeps going

      Left alone, it does not stop. It spreads, anchors deeper, and gets harder to remove.

    7. Treated, not blasted

      The fix is not more pressure. On most house surfaces, high pressure can cause damage, and still leave living growth behind. A soft wash treats the growth first, then rinses it away gently.

    8. Protect the surface

      A clean exterior is about more than looks. It protects the surfaces that protect your home.
