Gutter cleaning

Gutters only matter when it rains — which in South-East Queensland means they matter a lot, very suddenly.

How it’s done.

Leaves, seed pods, roof grit and nesting material build up quietly through the dry months. The first serious downpour then finds the water going over the edge, back under the fascia, or straight into a blocked downpipe.

We clear the run by hand where access allows, flush the line, and check that downpipes are actually taking water away. You get told what we found — including anything that looks like it needs a plumber or roofer rather than a cleaner.

Why it matters
Get Ready Queensland lists clearing gutters, downpipes and drains as a core step in preparing a home for storm season.

What’s included

  • Removal of leaf litter and debris from accessible runs
  • Downpipe outlets checked and flushed
  • Debris bagged and taken away
  • Photos of anything we think you should see
  • A note of any damage, rust or fall issues we spot

Surfaces we handle

  • Standard fascia gutters
  • Half-round and quad profiles
  • Box gutters where safely accessible
  • Downpipe outlets and leaf guards
  • Valley gutters, subject to access
Honest limits

Access decides a lot. Single-storey homes with reasonable ground access are straightforward. Two-storey, steep-pitch, tile-edge or hard-to-reach sections are assessed first, and we’ll decline anything that can’t be done safely rather than improvising.

Gutters, answered.

Common questions about gutter cleaning. More in the full FAQ.

When should gutters be cleaned in South-East Queensland?

Before the storm season — the Bureau of Meteorology treats October to April as the severe-weather season, with southern Queensland’s thunderstorm peak in October to December. Homes under heavy tree cover may need a second clean.

Do gutter guards mean I never need cleaning?

They reduce it rather than end it. Fine debris, roof grit and seed pods still collect on and under guards, and they need checking — especially before storm season.

Will insurance cover damage from blocked gutters?

Often not. Many home policies exclude damage caused by poor maintenance, and blocked gutters are a common example. Check your own policy wording.
