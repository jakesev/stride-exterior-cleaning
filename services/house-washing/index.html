House washing

Most house exteriors should never see high pressure. The job is to lift the growth off the surface, not force water behind it.

How it’s done.

Walls, eaves, soffits and cladding collect a fine layer of airborne dirt, cobwebs and organic growth — usually worst on the shaded southern side. High pressure on these surfaces risks driving water into joints, lifting paint and damaging render.

We use a low-pressure soft-wash approach instead: the surface is treated, given time to work, then rinsed at a pressure the material can take. It’s slower, and it’s the right way to do it.

Why it matters
Render maker Rockcote specifies about 500 psi through a fan tip for its systems — and warns that a direct needlepoint jet “may lift the sturdiest finish.”

What’s included

  • Assessment of the wall material and its condition
  • Plants, outdoor furniture and fittings considered before we start
  • Low-pressure application, dwell time, then a controlled rinse
  • Cobweb and nest removal from eaves and corners
  • A walk-around with you at the end

Surfaces we handle

  • Painted weatherboard and fibre cement
  • Rendered and bagged walls
  • Face brick and blockwork
  • Eaves, soffits, fascia and barge boards
  • Garage doors, downpipes and exterior trims
Honest limits

Existing damage doesn’t get better under water. Flaking paint, failed sealant, perished render and rotted timber are noted before work starts and may limit what’s possible. Where we think a surface shouldn’t be washed, we’ll say so.

House washing, answered.

Common questions about house washing. More in the full FAQ.

Will soft washing hurt my garden or pets?

Plants near the work are wetted down before and rinsed after, run-off is directed away from beds where practical, and pets should be inside while we work. Tell us about anything you’re especially attached to and we’ll plan around it.

Can you pressure wash render?

Only gently, and only if it’s sound. Render manufacturers specify low pressure through a fan tip; a concentrated jet can lift the finish or drive water into cracks. Soft washing does the cleaning chemically, so force isn’t needed.

How often should a house be washed?

Manufacturers of render and paint systems suggest light maintenance washes every one to two years. In practice the shaded, humid sides of a Redlands home usually show growth first, and that’s what sets the timing.
