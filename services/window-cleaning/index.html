Window cleaning

Glass is the one exterior surface people look through rather than at, which is why it shows neglect faster than anything else on the house.

How it’s done.

Exterior glass collects the same airborne film as the walls around it, plus salt closer to the bay and mineral deposit from sprinklers and run-off. Frames and tracks hold grit that scratches the glass if it is dragged across during cleaning.

Glass is cleaned with the frames and tracks, not around them. Where the exterior is being washed at the same time, windows are done after the walls so nothing settles back onto clean glass.

Why it matters
Glass maker Pilkington warns that sprinkler water left to dry on glass repeatedly can go beyond a surface deposit and etch the glass itself.

What’s included

  • Frames, sills and tracks cleaned, not just the glass
  • Sequenced after house washing where both are in scope
  • Screens handled carefully and refitted
  • Water-fed pole for upper-level glass where access allows
  • A check over the finished glass in daylight before we leave

Surfaces we handle

  • Exterior glass, ground and upper level within safe reach
  • Frames, sills and sliding tracks
  • Security screens, removed where practical
  • Glass balustrades and pool fencing
  • Louvres and awning windows
Honest limits

Cleaning removes what is on the glass. It does not remove what is in it. Mineral etching from long-term sprinkler contact, scratches, and the fine pitting that comes from age or salt exposure will not lift. Those are identified at the assessment so you know before rather than after.

Windows, answered.

Common questions about window cleaning. More in the full FAQ.

Why do my windows have white spots that won’t come off?

Usually mineral deposits from sprinkler or bore water. Fresh deposits clean off; long-standing ones can etch into the glass and become permanent. Redirecting sprinklers is the best prevention.

Are water-fed poles as good as traditional cleaning?

For exterior glass, yes, when done properly. Purified water dries without leaving mineral spots, and it lets upper-storey glass be cleaned from the ground. Frames and tracks are still detailed by hand.

Do you clean inside windows?

Our focus is exterior surfaces. If you need interior glass too, mention it in your quote request and we’ll tell you what’s possible.
