Solar panel cleaning

Panels are glass over an electrical system on a roof. That combination sets the method before anything else is considered.

How it’s done.

Dust, pollen, salt and bird droppings settle on panel glass and reduce the light reaching the cells. In South-East Queensland the dry months do most of the depositing and the first storm rarely clears it evenly, leaving streaks that shade the array.

Panels are cleaned with deionised water and a soft brush — no detergent, no pressure and nothing abrasive. Deionised water dries without leaving the mineral spotting that tap water leaves behind, which matters on a surface whose whole job is letting light through.

Why it matters
Panel manufacturers’ manuals generally call for soft water and gentle cleaning, and warn that pressure washing or harsh chemicals can damage modules — and the warranty.

What’s included

  • Deionised water and soft-brush method only
  • No detergent, no pressure, nothing abrasive on the glass
  • Array checked for obvious debris, nesting and shading
  • Anything visibly amiss reported to you, not touched
  • Work declined where the roof cannot be accessed safely

Surfaces we handle

  • Panel glass and frames
  • Array edges where debris collects
  • Roof surface immediately around the array
  • Accessible mounting rails
Honest limits

This is cleaning, not electrical work. Wiring, isolators, inverters, mounting integrity and anything under the panels are outside scope — if something looks wrong it is reported for your solar installer. Panels are never pressure washed and never cleaned in the heat of the day, when cold water on hot glass risks thermal shock.

Solar panels, answered.

Common questions about solar panel cleaning. More in the full FAQ.

Doesn’t rain clean solar panels?

Partly. Steady rain rinses loose dust, but light showers can leave residue, and bird droppings and lichen don’t wash off. Flat-mounted panels self-clean less well than steeply tilted ones.

How much output do dirty panels lose?

It varies widely with location, tilt and what’s on the glass. International research puts typical annual losses from soiling at a few per cent, with bird droppings causing bigger local losses because they shade whole cells.

Can I clean panels myself with a hose?

From the ground, a gentle hose rinse in the cool of the morning is generally fine. Avoid detergents, abrasive brushes and pressure washers, and don’t go on the roof — that’s where most of the risk is.
