Why location changes the job.

Close to the bay

Salt settles on sheltered surfaces that rain never reaches — eaves, gutter undersides, window frames. It needs washing off rather than waiting for the weather.

Under trees

Inland, bushland suburbs trade salt for leaf litter and shade: gutters fill faster and shaded paths and roof faces grow moss and lichen sooner.
