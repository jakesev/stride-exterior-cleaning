Cleaning is the job. Not damaging the property is the standard.

Concrete takes pressure. Render, weatherboard, fibre cement and natural stone do not — force drives water behind the surface, lifts coatings and etches soft stone, and none of that is undone by cleaning it again.

So every job starts with the surface: what it is, how old it is, what condition it’s in. Then the method follows — soft wash, surface cleaner, pure water or hand work — and you’re told what will lift and what will only lighten before anything is booked.

Before you book.

The questions people ask most. There are more in the full FAQ.

Can you quote from photos?

Usually, yes. Most driveways, house washes, gutters, windows and patios can be priced from a few clear phone photos plus your postcode. Roofs, solar, three-storey homes and anything with tricky access normally need a quick free visit first — the quote form tells you which applies before you send.

Do you use high pressure on every surface?

No. High pressure suits sound concrete and most paving. It is the wrong tool for render, painted weatherboard, fibre cement, timber, roof tiles and natural stone, where it drives water behind the surface, lifts coatings and etches soft stone. Those surfaces are soft-washed: a cleaning solution does the work at low pressure, is given time to dwell, then rinsed.

Will cleaning damage my render or paint?

Not when the method matches the material. Sound render and sound paint are unaffected by a correctly applied soft wash. Coatings that are already blistering, chalking or lifting can come away — the cleaning reveals a failure that was already there rather than causing it. We identify that before any work is booked.

Are you insured?

Yes. Stride carries $10 million public liability cover. The certificate of currency is available on request, and body corporates and property managers generally ask to see it before work starts — we’d rather you asked too.

Do I need to be home?

Not necessarily. On the day we need access to the areas being cleaned, an outdoor tap with reasonable flow, and a number to reach you on. Many customers are at work while we’re there. If you want to walk through it with us at the start or end, we’re happy to.

Which marks will not come out?

Cleaning removes what sits on a surface; it does not alter the surface itself. Oil that has soaked into porous concrete, rust bleed, efflorescence, paint spill and UV fade will usually lighten rather than disappear. Etching, spalling and delamination are physical damage. You’ll be told which category a mark falls into before the job is priced.
