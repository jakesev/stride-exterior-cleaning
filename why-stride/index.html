The Stride standard

The details matter.

Anyone can point a lance at a driveway. The difference is in the decisions made before the trigger is pulled — and in what happens when something doesn’t go to plan.

Get a free quote Request a booking
01

Surface-appropriate cleaning

Concrete can take a rotary surface cleaner at pressure. Render, weatherboard, natural stone and painted walls generally cannot. Using the wrong method forces water into joints, lifts paint and etches soft stone — damage that cleaning cannot undo. Working out which is which is the actual skill.

02

Insured, and happy to show it

$10 million public liability cover, with the certificate of currency available on request. Body corporates and property managers usually ask for it before work starts, and we’d rather you asked too.

03

Clear communication

A written scope, an arrival window rather than a vague day, and a message if anything changes. If we find something on site that changes the job, you hear about it then — not on the invoice.

04

Run-off taken seriously

In Queensland, outdoor cleaning water must not reach gutters or stormwater drains. We plan where water goes before we start, protect drains, and use only the chemistry a surface actually needs.

05

Before-and-after documentation

Where practical, surfaces are photographed before and after from the same position. It shows what improved and, just as importantly, what didn’t.

06

Respect for the property

Plants get considered, fittings get worked around, and the surrounding area gets rinsed down at the end. A clean driveway and a silt-covered garden bed is not a finished job.
