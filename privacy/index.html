Privacy policy

Stride Exterior Cleaning collects the minimum it needs to quote and carry out work, and doesn’t do anything else with it.

Last updated October 2026.

What we collect

  • Details you send us through the quote, booking, contact and guide forms: name, phone, email, property address or postcode, the services you’re interested in, preferred dates, notes, and any photos you choose to upload.
  • Basic technical information your browser sends when you visit, such as the pages requested. This site does not run advertising trackers.

Photos you upload

Photos are used only to understand and quote your job. They’re resized in your browser before upload and stored with your enquiry. Tell us if you’d like them deleted once the job is quoted or completed.

How we use it

  • To prepare and send you a quote, and to confirm bookings.
  • To carry out and record work you’ve booked (for warranty, insurance and tax purposes).
  • If you ask for them, to send the seasonal reminders you signed up for — every email has an unsubscribe link.

If an enquiry can’t be sent

If your connection drops while sending, a copy of the enquiry is kept on your own device (in your browser’s storage) so it can be retried. It is deleted once sent, or you can clear it with your browser’s site data.

Photographs of our work

Where practical we photograph surfaces before and after work. We may use these to show the standard of our work, with identifying details such as house numbers and number plates kept out of frame or removed. If you’d prefer your property wasn’t photographed or shown, tell us and we won’t — it’s never a condition of the work.

Who we share it with

We don’t sell your information and we don’t share it for marketing. It’s stored with the service providers we use to receive and manage enquiries, and shared otherwise only where necessary — for example with our insurer if a claim arises, or where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that don’t become jobs are kept for a reasonable period and then deleted. Records of completed work are kept as long as tax and insurance obligations require.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where we’re not required to keep it. Email info@strideexteriorcleaning.com.au.
